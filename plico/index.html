Plico Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Data controller: Padua Informatics
Contact: paduainformatics@gmail.com

Your documents

Photos and scans are processed and stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to third parties, and they are excluded from cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. They leave the app only when you choose to export or share them, to the destination you select. The same goes for the text recognised for in-app search and for the signature you save.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to take photos of documents, when you open the in-app camera.
  • Internet: used to show ads, to manage consent and for Google SDK diagnostics. Your documents are never sent.
  • Biometrics: only if you turn on the app lock. Your fingerprint, face or PIN is checked by Android: the app only receives the result and no biometric data.

Text and code recognition

The text of your documents is recognised for in-app search, searchable PDFs and Copy text; QR codes and barcodes are read when you ask. Both run on the device with Google ML Kit, using models bundled with the app: the images and text of your documents are not sent to Google or to us. The SDK may send technical and diagnostic data about the use of these features to Google.

Advertising

The app shows banners, a full-screen ad when it opens and, only if you choose to watch it to export a document of 4 or more pages, a rewarded video, through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). Google and its partners may process device data such as the advertising ID, IP address, technical information about the device and interactions with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The content of your documents is never provided to the ad network.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent choice, which you can change at any time under Privacy > Ad preferences. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Legal basis and retention

Personalised advertising is based on your consent; other advertising processing relies on the basis stated in the consent form. Documents stay on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Retention of advertising data is determined by Google.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction or objection, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. We do not store your data: for data processed by Google, see https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published here.
